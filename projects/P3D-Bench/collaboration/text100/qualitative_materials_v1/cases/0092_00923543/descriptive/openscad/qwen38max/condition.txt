The model is a low rectangular open web with a perimeter and internal diagonal and cross ribs rather than a filled slab. Several inner loops pass through the base, including the visible circular openings, so the open areas should remain voids. The two long side walls are added solid material along opposite edges, not cuts, and they rise higher than the thin web. The part has stepped depth: the web is shallow while the side walls extend to a greater height from the same general base side.
